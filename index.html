Cut(0)=5, Cut(1)=6, Cut(2)=7, Cut(3)=8, Cut(4)=9

🔥 Trick 3: Andar / Bahar (Haruf) System

2-digit number = Bahar (Left/Outer) + Andar (Right/Inner)

45 → Bahar=4, Andar=5
Both can be bet separately as "Haruf"

🔥 Trick 4: Munda & Bindi Special

0 = Munda | 1 = Bindi. These are special numbers:

Munda-Munda = 00 | Munda-Bindi = 01 | Bindi-Munda = 10

These repeat in cycles. Track 30-day history to find the pattern.

For result AB:

Sum = (A+B) mod 10 | Diff = |A-B|
Reverse = BA | Next possible = (Sum)(Diff)
